Print the blank recovery card, enter your 12 or 24 words here, and this page tells you exactly which squares to fill in, row by row. When you're done you'll have a Compact SeedQR your signing device can scan.

Before you type a single word

  • Disconnect from the internet. Turn off Wi‑Fi and unplug ethernet. Ideally use a computer that never goes online again, or a live USB OS.
  • This page can't phone home. It's a single file with no outside connections, and its security policy blocks every network request.
  • Nothing is saved. When you're done, hit Wipe, close the browser, and never print or photograph a filled-in grid.

01

Enter your words

Type the first 4 letters of each word. That's enough to identify any BIP39 word, and it auto-completes when you press Space, Enter or Tab. Pasting a whole phrase into box 1 fills every box.

02

Fill in the grid

Work one row at a time, top to bottom. Columns are lettered left to right (A is the far-left column, including the corner squares), rows are numbered top to bottom. Colour in only the squares listed. Leave everything else white.

  • Use a fine black felt-tip and colour each square right to the edges. Solid squares scan best.
  • Count from the nearest heavy line. They fall every 5 squares (after E, J, O, T).
  • Row 7 and column G hold the timing stripes, which alternate dark/light between the corner squares. Easy to sanity-check.

Need a blank grid?

A Compact SeedQR for 24 words must be exactly 25 × 25 squares, with the small alignment square 4 squares in from the right and bottom edges. Check your card against this before you start.

Print a correct blank grid at 100% scale (turn off "fit to page"). Only empty templates print. The filled-in grid never leaves the screen.
