Dice Seed Generator

Turn physical dice rolls into a BIP39 seed phrase, entirely offline.

Before you start · Network: checking…
  • Disconnect from wifi/ethernet (or use an airgapped computer) and open this file from disk.
  • Use a private/incognito window and turn off browser extensions.
  • Roll a real, fair die. Don't make up numbers, because people are bad at randomness.
  • Don't screenshot or photograph the words. With your camera covered, write them on paper or metal, then close this tab.

    How it works

    The rolls are joined into a text string (for example 3615…) and hashed with SHA-256. For 12 words the first 16 bytes of the hash become the entropy; for 24 words all 32 bytes do. The entropy is then encoded as a standard BIP39 mnemonic. This is the same method Coldcard and SeedSigner use for dice seeds, so you can enter the same rolls on one of those devices and check that the words match. If you choose "all but the last word", only the first 11/23 words are shown. Each of those words comes straight from the entropy bits. The final word holds a few more entropy bits plus the checksum, and your device works it out. The master fingerprint is the standard BIP32 one: the first 4 bytes of HASH160 of the master public key, derived from the words plus your optional passphrase. It identifies the wallet without revealing anything about the seed.
