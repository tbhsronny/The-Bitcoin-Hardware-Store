The rules that never change

Always

  1. Education first. Never skip learning the fundamentals.
  2. Start small. Test with small amounts before committing large sums.
  3. Multiple backups. Always keep redundant backups.
  4. Regular testing. Periodically rehearse your recovery procedure.
  5. Inheritance planning. Decide what happens to your bitcoin when you're gone.

Red flags

  • Storing seed phrases in digital photos or cloud storage
  • Using brain wallets or private keys you made up yourself
  • Trusting "bitcoin doublers" or yield-generating schemes
  • Sharing private keys or seed phrases with anyone
  • Using SMS for two-factor authentication
  • Keeping all your bitcoin in a single location or method
Choosing your level

Level 5 isn't the goal

Most bitcoin holders sit between Levels 1 and 3, and the majority are still at Level 1. Level 4 is for advanced users who take security seriously. Level 5 is for people with institutional-scale needs and the expertise to run it. Pick the level that matches your:
